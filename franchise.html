For Brand Owners

What a conversation with us looks like

  1. Introduction

    You tell us the brand, the territory on offer and the development expectations. We tell you honestly whether it fits our portfolio.

  2. Due diligence both ways

    We share company profile, audited financials, existing brand references and outlet performance. We ask for your FDD or equivalent, fee structure and support model.

  3. Market study

    A written feasibility: trade areas, competitor density, pricing sensitivity, labour costs and a five-year unit-economics model.

  4. Development plan

    Site count, opening schedule, capital source and the operating team assigned to the brand.

  5. Agreement and launch

    Contract, training, fit-out, certification, and a first opening we are both happy to put a photo of on the website.

For Suppliers

What we need before we can buy from you

Certification

Valid business registration, food-handling licences and, for food items, current lab analysis or a recognised food-safety certification.

Specification match

Product must meet the franchisor's written specification exactly — grade, size, packaging and shelf life included.

Consistency & capacity

Reliable volumes, stable pricing over an agreed term, and a delivery schedule that fits our outlets' receiving windows.

FAQ

Questions we are usually asked

Do you sell franchises of your own?

No. Royal Bliss (Pvt) Ltd is a franchisee — we operate other companies' brands under licence. We do not sub-franchise unless a brand owner specifically authorises it in our territory.

Which territories do you operate in?

Our current outlets and development pipeline are listed on the Brands & Outlets page. Territory rights vary by brand agreement, so it is best to ask us directly about a specific area.

How long does it take to open a new outlet?

Typically three to six months from a signed lease, depending on the format, landlord handover condition and the franchisor's design approval cycle. Kiosks are faster; full-kitchen restaurants take longer.

Can we discuss an exclusive supply agreement?

Yes, where the franchisor permits local sourcing for that item. Exclusivity is possible for suppliers who clear our audit and can hold price and quality over the term.

How do I report a problem with an order or an outlet?

Please use the contact form and select "Guest feedback", or call the outlet directly. Guest complaints are reviewed by the operations team, and anything food-safety related is escalated the same day.
